Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Operated by SYEDS ENTERPRISES, Hyderabad, India · Contact: syedsinterprises@gmail.com

Short version: this is a demo. Anything you type into it stays in your own browser for this visit only. Nothing is sent to a server, stored in an account, or shared with anyone. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts.

What this site is

NestoraBuy CRM is a demonstration build of a real-estate CRM (leads, pipeline, properties, follow-ups). The leads, properties and tasks shown when the page first loads are fictional sample entries so you can see how it works.

What data is collected

Leads and follow-ups you add. When you use the “Add a new lead” or “Add a follow-up” forms, the details you type (name, phone, email, what the lead wants, budget, follow-up notes) are kept in your browser’s memory for this visit only, so the page can show them back to you. They are never sent to any server, never stored in a database, and never shared with anyone. Closing the tab clears them. You can download them as a CSV file from the Leads tab at any time — that file stays on your device.

Nothing else. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, shows no ads, and asks you to create no account. It does not track you across other sites.

Third parties

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google may see your IP address, as with any website that loads Google Fonts. No other third-party service is loaded.

Children’s data

This demo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Your rights (India’s DPDP Act 2023)

Because nothing you enter is stored on our servers, there is no copy held by us to access, correct or delete — to remove what you entered, close the tab or clear your browser data. If you have any question about your data, write to syedsinterprises@gmail.com and we will answer it.

Changes to this policy

If this demo ever starts storing data on a server (for example, when a database is connected), this policy will be updated first to say so. The “last updated” date above will change when that happens.
